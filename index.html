Summary

Throughout my career of over 12 years, I’ve led multidisciplinary teams in designing products and services that transform how people interact with digital ecosystems. We’ve done this by uniting human-centered design principles, mixed-methods research, and emerging technologies like Artificial Intelligence.

This includes defining 0-to-1 product concepts and AI-driven experiences (at Google with Gemini and Workspace), building cultural localization strategies to adapt global features for the Brazilian market (at Meta with WhatsApp and Instagram), scaling super-app ecosystems and monetization models across Latin America (at Rappi with Rappi Ads and Rappibank), creating integrated urban logistics and micro-mobility networks (at Tembici with the iFood Pedal initiative), and mapping critical user journeys for banking and fintech leaders (at Nubank and Itaú).

Today, I serve as Director of UX Research & Design at Ipsos, leading a team of designers, researchers, and specialists. I am on a mission to connect consumer behavioral intelligence directly to strategic product roadmaps and go-to-market plans, turning human behavior into scalable revenue and high-performing products for global brands.
