Meta

Cultural localization for Brazil.

Adapting global features from WhatsApp and Instagram to the Brazilian market.

Company
Meta (via Ipsos)
Role
Director UX Research & Design
Period
2023 – Present
Location
São Paulo, Brazil

Overview

I structured cultural localization strategies and adapted Meta ecosystem products (WhatsApp & Instagram) for the Brazilian market.

Challenge

Features designed for a global audience don’t automatically fit local habits, language and culture. Without contextual relevance, adoption suffers.

My role

Leading research and design for the localization work, in partnership with Meta’s US HQ product teams.

Approach

Mixed-methods research with Brazilian users to understand local behavior and translate it into localization strategies.

Impact

Products adapted to the Brazilian context, ensuring contextual relevance and feature adoption.
