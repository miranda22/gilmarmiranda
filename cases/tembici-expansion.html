Tembici

Scaling LATAM’s largest micromobility network.

Turning research into operational improvements to support expansion across Brazil, Chile and Argentina.

Company
Tembici
Role
Manager UX Researcher
Period
May 2019 – April 2021
Location
São Paulo, Brazil

Overview

I translated qualitative data and physical/digital journey mapping into operational improvements for Tembici, LATAM's largest micromobility network.

Challenge

Expanding a bike-sharing network to new cities and countries means the service has to work in the street, not only in the app.

My role

Leading UX research across physical and digital touchpoints.

Approach

Qualitative research and physical/digital journey mapping, turned into concrete operational improvements.

Impact

Supported the expansion to over 300 active stations across 8 major cities in Brazil, Chile and Argentina.
