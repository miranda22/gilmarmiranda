Google · Meta

0-to-1 AI-driven products.

Defining new product concepts and AI-driven experiences with Google (Gemini, Workspace) and Meta (WhatsApp, Instagram) product teams.

Company
Google & Meta (via Ipsos)
Role
Director UX Research & Design
Period
2023 – Present
Location
São Paulo, Brazil · US HQ

Overview

As a UX Research & Design consultant at Ipsos, I led cross-functional teams in direct partnership with US headquarters (HQ) product teams at Google (Gemini & Workspace) and Meta (WhatsApp & Instagram) on 0-to-1 product conceptualization.

Challenge

Building a product from zero means there is no usage data to rely on. Teams need a clear understanding of real people, their contexts and needs before committing to a direction.

My role

Leading a local team of designers, researchers and simultaneous translators, working side by side with HQ product teams.

Approach

Mixed-methods research (qual/quant) across regional and global scopes, combining human-centered design principles with emerging AI technologies.

Impact

Research that steered strategic design and business decisions for new AI-driven products and experiences.
