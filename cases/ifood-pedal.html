Tembici · iFood

iFood Pedal.

A holistic support ecosystem for delivery couriers: e-bike rentals and rest zones.

Company
Tembici & iFood
Role
Manager UX Researcher
Period
May 2019 – April 2021
Location
São Paulo, Brazil

Overview

I co-led the strategy and field research for the creation of a holistic support ecosystem for couriers, including e-bike rentals and rest zones.

Challenge

Delivery couriers face physical, financial and logistical barriers every day. The goal was to design a service that worked for them and for both businesses.

My role

Co-leading strategy and field research.

Approach

Field research and physical/digital journey mapping with couriers.

Impact

The project directly increased delivery volume and combined revenue for both Tembici and iFood.
