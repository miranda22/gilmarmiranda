Context

Beta Testers is an exclusive Itaú program for testing products and features in production, with real customers, before large-scale launch. Ipsos was a partner in the program for 2 years.

The model keeps the team in continuous contact with users, collecting insights that support product decisions quickly and efficiently.

Objectives

  • Test and evaluate the quality of the experience and overall satisfaction with new products and features before large-scale launch.
  • Collect structured reports and feedback in real time, with groups of 15 to 150 customers engaged in a community.
  • Identify opportunities, evidence and remaining usability, performance and other issues that affect the user experience.
  • Mitigate bias in real contexts: testing across different devices and operating systems, geographic locations and a balanced mix of ages.

Process

Tests run in waves, with a focus on qualitative capture of how the solutions are actually used.

  • Fast iterations and collaboration with design and business teams.
  • Fast, proactive delivery of insights and of business and experience questions, which can be followed in a dashboard.

The 3 pillars

  1. Agility and feedback: a way to stay in continuous contact with users, collecting insights that support product decisions quickly and efficiently.
  2. Smaller-scale validation: evaluating the usability and performance of the experience in the real production environment before a larger rollout.
  3. Continuous insights: iterating and co-creating new hypotheses (experiments) with the design and business teams, based on continuously mapped opportunities.

Our role

On the Ipsos side, we had moderators and project managers serving the Itaú team.

We delivered about 28 projects in total, serving both B2B and B2C areas.
